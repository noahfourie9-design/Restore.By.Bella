Good to know

What should I wear?

Shorts or a sports bra for lower-body and back work, or whatever you are comfortable in. Towels and draping are provided.

How do I cancel or reschedule?

Use the link in your confirmation email. Please give at least [24 hours] notice so the slot can go to someone else.

How does payment work?

Pay by card at your session. Regular clients settle every second visit.

Is my booking confirmed straight away?

Not quite. Bella approves each request personally, and you will get an email when it is confirmed. If you need a slot urgently, send her a WhatsApp message.
